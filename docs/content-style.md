# Content Style Guide

Voice for Ayush Rai’s digital home. Use with `personal-positioning.md` and `content-fact-check.md`.

---

## 1. Voice attributes

| Attribute | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | Say the thing | “Built an MVP site for an agrotech brand.” |
| Specific | Names, dates, artifacts | “RaSoR–IITM, Dec 2023, internship.” |
| Calm | No hype ladder | Avoid “revolutionary,” “elite,” “5x.” |
| Honest | Label status | “In progress,” “prototype,” “client project.” |
| Evidence-first | Numbers only with proof | Otherwise qualitative |

**Tone:** Competent peer writing for other builders — not LinkedIn influencer, not corporate agency.

---

## 2. Person and tense

- Site chrome / about: **third person** or **first person** — pick one site-wide.  
  - **Recommendation:** First person on About/Connect (“I”), third-person short bio optional in footer/colophon.  
- Case studies: first person (“I built…”) or neutral (“The project…”) — be consistent within a page.  
- Prefer present tense for current roles; past for completed work.

---

## 3. Titles and identity

**Do:**

- One primary framing per surface.  
- Put secondary skills in context (“React and TypeScript on the client site”).  

**Don’t:**

- Stack titles with pipes: `SDE - AI Engineer | Generative AI Specialist | …`  
- Rotate headlines every 3 seconds.  
- Use “Polymath” as a job description.

---

## 4. Words to prefer / avoid

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| built, shipped, designed, measured, drafted | leveraged, utilized, spearheaded (unless literal) |
| prototype, MVP, in progress | revolutionary, next-gen, cutting-edge |
| selected work | comprehensive portfolio of excellence |
| collaboration | synergy |
| applied AI / RLHF pipelines | “worked with OpenAI” (unless carefully true) |
| messy constraints | pain points (overused) |

**Banned on public pages until verified:** any exact percentage impact, dollar funding, “N+ students reached,” “100%,” “24/7.”

---

## 5. Structure patterns

### Home supporting sentence

≤25 words. No metrics. No title list.

### Section intro

One headline + one sentence. Then content. No second paragraph of throat-clearing.

### Work summary (card)

2 sentences max:

1. What it is / who for  
2. What you did or status  

### Case study body

Short sections with plain headings:

- Context  
- Problem  
- Approach  
- Outcome (honest)  
- Links  

### Experience bullet

Start with a verb. One line. No nested brag lists.

✅ `Maintained and updated research web apps for RaSoR–IITM.`  
❌ `Dynamically leveraged cutting-edge best practices to deliver transformative value.`

---

## 6. Metrics style

When a metric is **verified**:

- State scope: “In a pilot with 20 farmers (spring 2024)…”  
- State method briefly if non-obvious.  
- Link evidence when possible.  

When **unverified**:

- Delete the number.  
- Or replace with process language: “Evaluated recommendations against field feedback.”  

Never invent precision (“~85%”) to look careful.

---

## 7. Names and formatting

- **Ayush Rai** — full name on first mention per page; “Ayush” OK after.  
- Orgs: use official capitalization (IIT Madras, FoCDoT, UiPath).  
- Degrees: “B.Tech in Computer Science,” “BS in Data Science and Applications.”  
- Dates: `Apr 2024–Present`, en dash, consistent month abbreviations.  
- Places: `Bhopal, India`.  
- Product names: **The Open Framework**, **AyushMushrooms** — bold sparingly on first mention only if needed for scan.

---

## 8. Links and CTAs

- Link text describes destination: “View demo,” “GitHub repository,” not “click here.”  
- External: open in new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.  
- Primary CTA verbs: View, Read, Email, Connect.  
- Avoid “Hire me now” agency energy; “Email me” is enough.

---

## 9. Privacy & safety in copy

- Public: email, city/country, professional links.  
- Not public: phone, personal address, private Drive links that expose PII, client secrets.  
- Client work: get permission; offer anonymization (“professional site for a leadership consultant”).  

---

## 10. Services / collaboration copy

If mentioning collaboration:

> I’m open to collaborations on applied AI systems, product builds with real users, and research that needs engineering to ship.

No price list. No package tiers. No “starting at $2000.”

---

## 11. Microcopy

| Element | Style |
| --- | --- |
| Form errors | Plain: “Enter a valid email.” |
| Empty work filter | “No entries in this category.” |
| In progress badge | `In progress` — not `Coming soon!!!` |
| Footer | Quiet colophon: name, year, built with … |

---

## 12. Editing checklist (before publish)

- [ ] One clear idea per section  
- [ ] No title stacking  
- [ ] No unverified numbers  
- [ ] Status labels accurate  
- [ ] Phone absent  
- [ ] Adjectives earn their keep or get cut  
- [ ] Reads fine aloud in ~20 seconds for home hero
